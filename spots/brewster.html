Brewster Flats

Brewster MA · Cape Cod · Striped Bass

Read the water

Two miles of hard sand that empties at low. Bass slide up the guzzles with the new flood and cruise in a foot of water like bonefish with shoulders.

The cast

Set up on a bar edge, sun behind you, and take shots at cruising singles. Long lead, soft landing, one strip when the fish is inside six feet of the fly.

Rig

8wt, floating line, 12ft leader, crab and sand eel flies #4 to #1. Quiet feet, no wading skirts of spray.

Prime time

First two hours of incoming, mid-morning through afternoon for sun, wind under 10.

When it blows out

Cloud kills the sight game. Blind the guzzle mouths on the same tide or run to the Canal turn.

Bait

Sand eels in the guzzles, green crabs on the bars, silversides against the grass edges.

Access (Public, sticker lots)

Town landings (Paines Creek, Point of Rocks) have resident sticker rules in summer, arrive at dawn or bike in.

Wading (Easy)

Walk out on the drop, fish the first of the flood, and keep a dry line home. The tide crosses this flat faster than you walk.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
