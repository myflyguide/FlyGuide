FLYGUIDE

Cape Poge Gut

Chappaquiddick, Marthas Vineyard MA · Cape & Islands · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The most storied hardtail water in New England. Cape Poge Bay drains through one gut, bait pours out on every tide, and bonito then albies then bass line up to collect.

The cast

Position down-current of the mouth, cast into the flow, and let the fly swing through the seam. The fish patrol a beat, if a school passes without eating, they will be back in ten minutes.

Rig

9wt, clear intermediate, 12ft to 16 or 20lb fluoro, Surf Candy and small squid flies. Fast hands beat fast fly lines here.

Prime time

Late July for bonito, September for albies and bass together, any moving tide, best at first light.

When it blows out

Northeast wind puts chop on the mouth. East Beach outside or the bay flats inside fish the same permit.

Bait

Silversides and sand eels flushing the bay, bay anchovies balled at the mouth from August, squid in early summer.

Access (OSV or long walk)

Chappy ferry, then Dike Bridge and the Trustees oversand trail to the Gut and elbow. Times here are estimates, the Gut runs on its own schedule.

Wading (Easy at the edges)

Sand wading beside the Gut is easy, the channel itself sluices Cape Poge Bay in and out twice a day. Fish the edges, respect the middle.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
