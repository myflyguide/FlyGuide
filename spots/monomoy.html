Monomoy Flats

Chatham MA · Cape Cod · Striped Bass

Read the water

The famous ocean flats. Big schools of bass flood across white sand in gin water and you get bonefish-style shots at 20 pound fish.

The cast

Sun high, wind at your back, lead the school and let them find it. One fish peeling off the edge is your target, not the middle of the pack.

Rig

9wt, floating, 12ft leader to 20lb, sand eel and crab flies. Bring more backing than pride.

Prime time

June and July, incoming water, 10am to 3pm sun window.

When it blows out

Fog is the killer out here and it comes fast. If it socks in, fish the Chatham inlets on the drop.

Bait

Sand eels are the whole story, with squid pulses in June out on the rips.

Access (Boat smart)

The outside flats are a boat or shuttle ride from Chatham. Rip Ryder shuttle runs from Outermost Harbor in season. Tide times here are estimates off Boston.

Wading (Easy once there)

Hard sand wading for miles once the boat drops you. Watch the tide, the bars flood from behind.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
