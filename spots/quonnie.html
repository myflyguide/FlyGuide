Quonochontaug Breachway

Charlestown RI · Rhode Island · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The quieter breachway. Same granite chute physics as Charlestown, same night game on the outgoing, plus pond flats inside that sight-fish on summer floods.

The cast

Drift flies down the outgoing seam at night, two hours either side of low. Fall daylight, watch the mouth for albies pinning bait against the beach.

Rig

9wt, intermediate, black flies at night, shrimp and worm patterns inside, epoxy anchovies for the fall visitors.

Prime time

Night outgoings June through September, fall mornings for the mixed bag.

When it blows out

This coast loves ugly. If flat calm kills the night bite, the pond flats at dawn are the counterpunch.

Bait

The pond exports shrimp, crabs, and silversides every drop, cinder worms inside around the May moons, anchovies in fall.

Access (Public)

State ramp lot at the end of West Beach Rd. The granite chute and pond-side flats are steps away, the crowd is thinner than Charlestown's.

Wading (Moderate)

Fish the rocks and the beach corners. The chute itself is a no-wade zone, the outgoing runs like a sluice.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
