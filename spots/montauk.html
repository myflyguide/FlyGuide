Montauk Point

Montauk NY · Long Island NY · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The End. Every migrating fish on the coast rounds this point, and in October the white water under the Light turns into the best surf blitz on earth.

The cast

Albies first light off the north bar: small anchovy fly, two-hand strip. Bass in the wash: white Hollow Fleye swung through the boulders. Stay mobile, the bait moves and you move with it.

Rig

10wt, intermediate plus a 350 grain, anchovy flies #2, big white profiles for bass. Korkers, drysuit-tough waders, a stripping basket or lose your line to the rocks.

Prime time

Mid-September through early November, moving tide at dawn.

When it blows out

It takes a real gale to kill Montauk, the fish just switch sides. North wind fish the south side, south wind fish the north bar.

Bait

Bay anchovies and peanut bunker in the fall blitz, sand eels spring, squid on the reefs.

Access (Public, fee lot)

State park lot at the Light. Fall weekends are a circus, fish weekdays or dawn.

Wading (Tricky)

Slick boulders under the Light, studded boots not optional. The north bar wades at moderate stages.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
