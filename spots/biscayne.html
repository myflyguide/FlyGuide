FLYGUIDE

Biscayne Bay

Miami FL · Miami · Bonefish, Tarpon

Read the water

Ten pound bonefish with a skyline backdrop. The bay's grass flats hold big, smart, boat-shy fish that reward the quiet angler on foot.

The cast

These bones have PhDs. Longer leads than the Bahamas, softer landings, smaller flies. When one tips on the fly, one slow strip and pray.

Rig

8wt, 12ft leader to 12lb fluoro, tan and olive shrimp flies #4 to #6, lighter eyes than you think.

Prime time

April through June and October, incoming water mid-morning, sun high, wind under 12.

When it blows out

Dirty water after a blow kills the sight game. Baby tarpon in the leeward creeks and canals save the day.

Bait

Shrimp and small crabs on the flood, glass minnows against the shorelines in summer, mullet in fall.

Access (Public edges, park fees)

Key Biscayne shorelines (Bill Baggs, Crandon) wade legally, Biscayne NP flats south are skiff water. City skyline, world-class bones.

Wading (Easy on the edges)

Hard sand and grass edges off the Key Biscayne parks wade well on the lower half. The middle bay is boat water.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
