FLYGUIDE

Brenton Point

Newport RI · Rhode Island · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

Newport's open-ocean corner. Deep water against the rocks, squid-eating bass in June, and a front-row seat when the fall albie parade rounds the point.

The cast

Count sinking lines down along the ledge edges and swim squid flies deep. In fall, hold your water and cast when the showers come through, they always come back.

Rig

10wt, 350 grain for the bass game, floating with anchovy flies for albies, a long-handled net or a plan for the rocks.

Prime time

June squid run at dawn, September October moving tides for albies and bass.

When it blows out

Real swell makes the ledges lethal, stand down. Inside Narragansett Bay at Fort Adams fishes the same day safely.

Bait

Squid on the reefs in early summer, sand eels and silversides in the coves, bay anchovies streaming past in fall.

Access (Public)

Brenton Point State Park, free lots on Ocean Drive. The rock shelves west of the lot are the fly perches.

Wading (Bank only)

Rock shelf casting over deep water, no wading. Swell surges these ledges, never turn your back on the ocean here.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
