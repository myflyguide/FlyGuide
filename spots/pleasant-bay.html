Pleasant Bay & Nauset Inlet

Orleans MA · Cape Cod · Striped Bass

Read the water

A huge protected bay behind the outer beach, drained by an inlet that rearranges itself every winter. Flats fish inside, rip fish at the throat, seals and sharks as neighbors.

The cast

Inside, sight-fish the bars on the flood like Monomoy's little brother. At the inlet, swing the seams on the drop from the safe side of the bar.

Rig

9wt covers both games, floating for the flats, intermediate at the inlet, sand eel flies always.

Prime time

June and July on the flats, September at the inlet, moving water at first light.

When it blows out

Ocean swell owns the inlet. The inside bay stays calm and fishes through nearly anything.

Bait

Sand eels on every bar, silversides in the bay grass, squid in the inlet some Junes, peanuts by September.

Access (Public edges, boat smart)

Nauset Beach lot (Orleans fee) for the inlet walk, town landings for the bay. The inside flats and the shifting inlet are best reached by skiff or a long committed walk.

Wading (Easy where sandy)

Hard sand around the inlet bars and outer bay edges. The inlet itself shifts yearly and the current is deadly serious, wade the edges, never the throat.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
