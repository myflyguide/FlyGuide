FLYGUIDE

Mosquito Lagoon

Titusville FL · East Central FL · Redfish

Read the water

The sight-fishing lab. No real tide, gin water on calm days, and big resident redfish that have seen everything, laid up like brown logs over white sand.

The cast

Stealth is the whole game. Push pole or wade quiet, lead laid-up fish by three feet, let the fly sit, then barely crawl it. If the fish flinches, stop everything.

Rig

7 or 8wt floating, 12ft leader, small weedless shrimp and crab flies #4, black/purple on grass, tan on sand.

Prime time

Any calm clear morning year-round, winter especially when the water cleans up.

When it blows out

Wind is everything here since there is no tide to save you. Over 12 knots, fish the lee shore of the spoil islands or go tie flies.

Bait

Shrimp and crabs in the grass, finger mullet on the shorelines, small pinfish in the potholes.

Access (Public, refuge rules)

Ramps at Haulover Canal and Biolab Rd inside Merritt Island NWR, check refuge hours. The lagoon is microtidal, wind moves more water than the moon here.

Wading (Easy where firm)

Sand shorelines wade fine, interior mud does not. The kayak is the great equalizer here.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
