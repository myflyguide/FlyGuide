The Marls

Abaco, Bahamas · Abaco · Bonefish

Read the water

An endless maze of creeks and flats holding uncountable bonefish in the 2 to 5 pound class, with enough doubles and school shots to fix a lifetime of bad casting.

The cast

Schools push wakes you can see at 200 feet. Lead the wake, let the fly land ahead, and strip when the lead fish is close. Fast, fun, forgiving.

Rig

8wt floating, 10ft to 12lb, Gotchas and pink shrimp #4 to #6, bead chain eyes for the soft bottoms.

Prime time

March through June, tide matters less here than light, fish it whenever the sun is up.

When it blows out

The Marls ARE the blowout plan, the mangroves kill the wind. This is where Abaco guides run when everything else is white-capped.

Bait

Shrimp, small crabs, and gobies in the mangrove edges. The fish here eat happily, which is why it is the best classroom in the sport.

Access (Lodge/guide water)

Two hundred square miles of mangrove flats on Abaco's west side, reached by skiff from Marsh Harbour lodges. Flats permit required, bones are catch and release nationwide.

Wading (Soft, boat based)

Mostly too soft to wade, this is poling water. Your guide will tell you the firm spots.

Regulations change: Bahamas flats fishing regulations (license required, bones are C&R).
Since 2017 the Bahamas requires a flats fishing permit for everyone, and bonefish are catch and release. DIY wading is legal on most flats but some are guide-only by local agreement. Ask at the lodge or settlement before you walk somebody's home water, it buys you more than goodwill.
