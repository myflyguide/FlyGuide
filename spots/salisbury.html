FLYGUIDE

Salisbury Beach & North Jetty

Salisbury MA · North Shore MA · Striped Bass

Read the water

The north side of the Merrimack mouth, the workingman's answer to Joppa. The jetty seam and the beach corner collect fish on every outgoing.

The cast

Cast up-current from the jetty and swing the seam, or work the corner where beach meets rocks. Night tides in June produce the biggest fish of the year here.

Rig

9 or 10wt, 300 grain in the current, black Deceivers after dark, sand eels by day.

Prime time

Outgoing tide, dawn, dusk, and after dark, May through October.

When it blows out

Northeast swell shuts the jetty. The inside river bends by the ramp fish the same water safely.

Bait

Everything the Merrimack grows: herring in spring, sand eels and peanut bunker in fall, sea worms in the May suds.

Access (Public, state fee)

Salisbury Beach State Reservation, camp side lots put you at the Merrimack's north jetty. Night fishing tolerated with a line in the water.

Wading (Bank and beach)

Fish the jetty rocks and the beach corner. The Merrimack outflow has drowned better swimmers than you, stay out of the channel.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
