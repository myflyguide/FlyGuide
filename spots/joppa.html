FLYGUIDE

Joppa Flats

Newburyport MA · North Shore MA · Striped Bass

Read the water

The Merrimack pours nutrients over miles of hard sand. Bass ride the flood over the bars in skinny water, often visibly pushing wakes.

The cast

This is New England sight fishing. Watch for nervous water and wakes, lead the push by ten feet, small sand eel fly, do not line them.

Rig

8 or 9wt, floating or clear intermediate, sand eel patterns #2 to 1/0, 12ft leader. Crab fly for fish rooting the bottom.

Prime time

Midday flood tides May through July, sun high so you can see, wind under 12.

When it blows out

Northeast blow muddies the river plume. Swing the state ramp side of the jetties on the drop instead.

Bait

Sand eels all season, juvenile menhaden by July, shrimp on the flood edges in June.

Access (Public)

Free lot at Joppa Park on Water St. MA intertidal is private to low water but fishing access is protected under the colonial ordinance.

Wading (Easy)

Flats wade easy, fish arrive with the flood. Know your exit line before the bars cover.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
