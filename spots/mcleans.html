FLYGUIDE

East End

McLeans Town, Grand Bahama · Grand Bahama · Bonefish

Read the water

Big-fish water an easy flight from Florida. Ocean-side flats hold heavy singles and doubles that eat crabs like they are paid to.

The cast

Crab fly fishing: lead the fish, let it hit bottom with a puff, and do not move it. The bone will race over and pin it. Moving a crab away from a bonefish is how you lose the shot.

Rig

8 or 9wt, 12ft to 16lb, #2 to #4 crab and mantis patterns with lead eyes, tan and olive.

Prime time

March through June, tide changes, mid-morning light.

When it blows out

North wind in winter is the enemy. The creeks and the south side pine flats give shelter and honest schoolies.

Bait

Crabs and mantis shrimp on the ocean flats, schools of mudding fish inside.

Access (Guide smart, some DIY)

The famous east end flats and pine cays run from McLeans Town, an hour from Freeport. Deep Water Cay's old water, big fish, guides know the tides cold.

Wading (Mixed)

Ocean side sand wades firm, the inside marl is soft. Guides stake and wade the good stuff daily.

Regulations change: Bahamas flats fishing regulations (license required, bones are C&R).
Since 2017 the Bahamas requires a flats fishing permit for everyone, and bonefish are catch and release. DIY wading is legal on most flats but some are guide-only by local agreement. Ask at the lodge or settlement before you walk somebody's home water, it buys you more than goodwill.
