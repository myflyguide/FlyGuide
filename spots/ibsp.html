FLYGUIDE

Island Beach State Park

Seaside Park NJ · New Jersey · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The most fly-friendly stretch on the Jersey coast: bars, sloughs, and cuts that reshape every storm, and a fall run that parks fish in the wash for weeks.

The cast

Find the cut in the outer bar, stand up-current of it, and swing sand eel flies through the funnel. November midday blitzes here are not a rumor.

Rig

9wt, intermediate, sand eel flies #1, white Deceivers, and the albie box through October.

Prime time

October through Thanksgiving, any moving tide, dawn and the last two hours of light.

When it blows out

Big blow rearranges the bars, which is a gift: fish it the first calm day and read the new cuts before the crowd does.

Bait

Sand eels are the fall headline, rain bait against the beach in October, mullet in September, bunker schools passing all season.

Access (Public, fee)

State park entrance fee, buggy corridor with permit. Ten miles of undeveloped beach, walk past the first cut and you are alone.

Wading (Easy)

Easy sand for ten miles. Read the bars at low, fish them at first light.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
