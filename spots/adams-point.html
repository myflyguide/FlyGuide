FLYGUIDE

Adams Point

Durham NH · NH Seacoast · Striped Bass

Read the water

The pinch point of the whole Great Bay system. Every herring in May and every peanut in September squeezes through Furber Strait, and the bass set up in the current seams to take a toll.

The cast

Swing the seams off the point on the outgoing like a river fisherman. When bait showers at the surface in fall, a peanut fly stripped through the melee gets crushed.

Rig

9wt, intermediate or sink tip in the deeper seam, herring patterns in spring, small bunker flies in fall.

Prime time

May and again September, first half of the outgoing, early and late in the day.

When it blows out

Almost weatherproof, the bay kills the swell. Mid-summer warm water is the real blowout, fish the coast instead in August.

Bait

River herring through the narrows in May, silversides and shrimp all summer, juvenile bunker exiting the bay in September.

Access (Public)

UNH Jackson Lab lot at the end of Adams Point Rd. The narrows between Little Bay and Great Bay funnel everything through one cut. Tides run about two hours behind the coast, corrected here.

Wading (Moderate)

Rocky shoreline wades at lower and mid stages. The mud coves on either side will keep a boot as a souvenir.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
