FLYGUIDE

Little Harbor

New Castle NH · NH Seacoast · Striped Bass

Read the water

The Seacoast's quiet sight fishery. Protected water behind New Castle where singles slide onto the flats with the flood and tail on crabs like little bonefish.

The cast

Sun high, move slow, look for wakes and tails on the young flood. Lead cruising fish ten feet with a crab or shrimp fly and let them find it.

Rig

8wt, floating, 12ft leader, crab flies #4 and small shrimp patterns. Quiet feet matter more than fly choice.

Prime time

First half of the flood, midday sun, May through July before it warms.

When it blows out

Cloud kills the sighting. Blind the channel edge on the same tide or slide to the Piscataqua turns.

Bait

Silversides and shrimp on the flats, juvenile herring in the channel, green crabs everywhere.

Access (Public)

Park at Odiorne's north lot or the Wentworth-Coolidge mansion side. The protected flats and channel edges sit behind the Wentworth hotel.

Wading (Easy)

Firm sand and mud flats wade well on the lower half. Know where the channel is before the flood erases the edges.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
