FLYGUIDE

Crane Beach & Essex Bay

Ipswich MA · North Shore MA · Striped Bass

Read the water

White sand bars where Essex Bay empties past Castle Neck, and bass cruise the guzzles in skinny water. The North Shore's answer to Brewster.

The cast

Low tide, walk the bars, take shots at fish sliding up the guzzles with the new flood. Long leads, small flies, one strip when they are close.

Rig

8 or 9wt, floating or clear tip, sand eel flies #4 to #1, crab fly for tailers.

Prime time

First two hours of flood with midday sun, June and July.

When it blows out

Cloud or chop ends the sight game. Blind the Essex River channel on the drop instead.

Bait

Sand eels by the acre on the bars, silversides and shrimp draining Essex Bay, sea worms in the spring.

Access (Public, steep fee)

Trustees lot at Crane Beach, pricey in summer, cheap at dawn. The Essex Bay side and Castle Neck bars are a walk down the beach.

Wading (Easy)

Miles of hard sand bars at the Essex Bay mouth. The flood comes fast across them, keep the exit in mind. Greenheads in late July take a blood toll.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
