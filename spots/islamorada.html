Islamorada Oceanside

Islamorada FL · Upper Keys · Tarpon, Bonefish

Read the water

The sport's cathedral. Migrating tarpon string down the oceanside edge April through June, laid-up fish in the basins, bones on the flats between.

The cast

Ocean side is a string game: see them early, lead the lead fish, fly at eye level, slow strips. If the first fish flushes, stay down, number three might eat.

Rig

12wt floating for tarpon, 60lb shock. 8wt with tan crab flies for the bones. Push-pole quiet, trolling motors flush these fish.

Prime time

Late April through mid-June, early flood, first three hours of light. Worm moons are the lottery.

When it blows out

East wind over 15 puts chop on the ocean edge. Flip to the bayside basins for laid-up fish in the lee.

Bait

Palolo worms around the May and June full moons, pilchards and crabs on the flood, glass minnows against the beach in summer.

Access (Public edges, boat smart)

Anne's Beach and roadside pulloffs give wade access to oceanside flats. The full show (Buchanan Bank, the edges) is skiff water. Tides are estimates off Vaca Key.

Wading (Easy where sandy)

Hard sand and marl off Anne's Beach wades fine on lower stages. Soft spots grab you near the grass, test as you go.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
