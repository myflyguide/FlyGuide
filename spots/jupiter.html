Jupiter Beaches

Jupiter FL · Southeast FL · Tarpon, Snook

Read the water

The palm beach tarpon sidewalk. Spring and fall, strings of fish cruise the clear trough heading north or south, visible from the dune line.

The cast

Walk high on the berm to spot, drop down to cast. Lead the string, let the fly sink to their level, slow draw. Snook eat the same water at your feet at first light.

Rig

11 or 12wt floating for the tarpon lane, 8wt for dawn snook, black/purple early then tan and white.

Prime time

April through June northbound, September October southbound with the mullet, always the first two hours of light.

When it blows out

East swell over three feet stirs the trough. The Loxahatchee River mangrove game inside is the fallback.

Bait

Spring glass minnow and pilchard runs, summer threadfins, the September mullet run with tarpon and snook stacked under it.

Access (Public)

Beach accesses off A1A from Juno to Hobe Sound, meter parking. The fish run the trough within casting range of dry sand.

Wading (Easy)

Stand on the sand, the fish are inside the bar. Wading out just puts you behind them.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
