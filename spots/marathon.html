Marathon Bridges

Marathon FL · Middle Keys · Tarpon

Read the water

Tarpon highway. Fish stack in the bridge shadow lines and channel edges on every moving tide, rolling like porpoises when they are happy.

The cast

From the boat, hold up-current and swing a black/purple fly down the shadow line. Night tides under the lights: small shrimp fly, dead drift, brutal eats.

Rig

11 or 12wt, intermediate or 350 grain for the channels, 80lb shock around structure because you will need every pound.

Prime time

April through July, the two hours around a tide change, dawn, dusk, and after dark.

When it blows out

Wind barely matters in the channels. If the ocean is up, the bayside bridge edges stay fishable.

Bait

Pilchards and mullet stacking the shadow lines, crabs flushing on the spring tides, worms on the May moons.

Access (Public)

Old Seven Mile Bridge and channel edges, walk-on access from Knights Key side and Pigeon Key path. Boat opens every channel.

Wading (Bank only)

This is structure fishing from the old bridge or a skiff, not a wade. Current in the channels is serious.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
