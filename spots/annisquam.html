FLYGUIDE

Annisquam & Wingaersheek

Gloucester MA · North Shore MA · Striped Bass

Read the water

The Annisquam empties past a white sand beach and the bass ride the channel edge like a moving sidewalk. Sight fishing at low, current fishing as it builds.

The cast

At low, hunt the bar edges for cruisers. As the flood builds, swing flies along the channel drop where the river current pins the bait.

Rig

8 or 9wt, floating for the bars, intermediate for the channel edge, sand eel patterns.

Prime time

Bottom of the tide through the first hours of flood, morning light, June through September.

When it blows out

It is remarkably protected. A hard northerly is the only wind that hurts, and Crane across the bay fishes the lee.

Bait

Sand eels on the bars, silversides riding the river, mackerel off the lighthouse some Junes.

Access (Public, fee lot)

Wingaersheek Beach lot (Gloucester fee) fronts the Annisquam River mouth. The bars and channel edge are right there at low water.

Wading (Easy)

Hard sand bars wade beautifully at the bottom of the tide. The channel drops off sharp, fish the edge, do not cross it.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
