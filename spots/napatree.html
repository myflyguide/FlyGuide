Napatree Point

Watch Hill RI · Rhode Island · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

A mile of sand ending in rips where Fishers Island Sound meets the ocean. Bass work the bars the whole walk, albies wrap the point in fall.

The cast

Fish your way out: cast to the bar edges every hundred yards. At the point, swing the rip seam and watch up-current for albie showers moving down the beach.

Rig

9wt, intermediate, sand eel flies and a Surf Candy box. Fall mornings carry both rods if you own them.

Prime time

September and October, first light, moving water at the point.

When it blows out

Southerly gale puts swell on it. The Weekapaug and Quonnie breachways fish the outgoing in the lee.

Bait

Silversides and sand eels along the bars, bay anchovies pinned at the point in September, squid in the Sound current.

Access (Public, paid village lots)

Park in Watch Hill village and walk the spit. The walk filters the crowd, worth every step.

Wading (Easy)

Easy sand the length of the spit. The point current deserves respect on big tides.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
