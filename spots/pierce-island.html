FLYGUIDE

Pierce Island

Portsmouth NH · NH Seacoast · Striped Bass

Read the water

A city-park flat in the middle of the Piscataqua where the current pins bait against slack water. When terns start dropping and fish start rising off the corner, it is happening right now, not in an hour.

The cast

Watch the birds and the surface first, cast second. Work the seam where the flat's slack meets the channel current, and when fish show on top, a small silverside fly swum through the melee gets eaten.

Rig

8 or 9wt, floating or intermediate, silverside and shrimp flies #2 to #1, a Gurgler when they are up. The current does the retrieve half the time.

Prime time

Flood tide pushing bait onto the flat, dawn and dusk, May through September. Unlike the rivers, the cold Piscataqua keeps this alive through summer.

When it blows out

The island blocks most wind. If the flat is dead, the tide turn at Fort Constitution is ten minutes east.

Bait

River herring through in May, silversides and shrimp pinned on the flat all season, peanut bunker stacked against it in September. The birds tell you when it is on.

Access (Public, free)

City park at the end of Pierce Island Rd with a free lot and ramp, five minutes from downtown Portsmouth. The flat and the back channel are steps from the truck.

Wading (Careful)

The flat off the southeast corner wades on the lower half with careful feet. The edges drop into serious Piscataqua current, stay on the flat, never chase a fish off it.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
