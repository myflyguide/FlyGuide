Boca Grande

Boca Grande FL · Southwest FL · Tarpon, Snook

Read the water

Thousands of tarpon stage here May and June. The fly game is the beach: strings run the bar line in clean water at first light before the pass fleet fires up.

The cast

Anchor or pole the beach bar, watch up-light for strings, lead the lead fish by fifteen feet. On the hill tides throw a crab fly and dead drift it like the real thing.

Rig

12wt floating, 60 to 80lb shock, tan and rust crab flies for the flushes, Toads for the strings.

Prime time

May and June, hill tides around the moons, dawn beach patrol.

When it blows out

West wind over 15 muddies the beach line. Charlotte Harbor's east side flats hold laid-up fish in the lee.

Bait

The famous hill tide crab flushes around the May and June moons, threadfins on the beach, mullet in fall.

Access (Public beaches, boat for the pass)

Gasparilla Island beaches wade free with paid island parking. The pass itself is the most famous tarpon hole on earth and strictly boat water.

Wading (Easy on the beach)

Beach wading is easy sand. The pass is 70 feet deep with hill tide current, nobody wades it, ever.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
