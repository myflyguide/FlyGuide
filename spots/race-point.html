FLYGUIDE

Race Point

Provincetown MA · Cape Cod · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

Deep water sweeps a sand point and the rip runs within a fly cast of dry sand. Fish feed at your bootlaces here more than anywhere in the Northeast.

The cast

Cast up-current into the rip line and swing it like a wet fly. Two-hand retrieve when they chase, dead drift when they are on sand eels thick.

Rig

9 or 10wt, intermediate or 300 grain, sand eel flies and white Deceivers. Fall run: bring the albie box too, they crash bait inside the bar.

Prime time

Moving tide at first light, June and again September into October. Mind the seals, where they stack the sharks patrol.

When it blows out

Big northeast swell closes it out. Herring Cove around the corner fishes the lee.

Bait

Sand eels by the acre, mackerel in June, peanut bunker and anchovies in the fall sweep.

Access (Public, fee)

National Seashore lot, entrance fee, ORV corridor with permit. The rip is a short walk west of the lot.

Wading (Easy)

Easy sand, but the bar drops into deep green water fast. Ankle to knee deep is plenty, the fish are at your feet.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
