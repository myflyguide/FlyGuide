Cape Cod Canal

Sandwich MA · Cape Cod · Striped Bass

Read the water

The Big Ditch. A seven mile river of ocean that reverses every six hours, and when the east tide lines up with dawn the biggest bass in the Northeast eat on top.

The cast

Honest talk: fly water here means the tide turns and the edges. 450 grain head, cast up-current, let it swing the seam at your feet. Breaking fish at range belong to the jig guys.

Rig

10wt minimum, 450 grain, short 30lb leader, big Hollow Fleye or squid fly. A bike on the service road doubles your water.

Prime time

East tide through first light, June and July around the moons when the big fish transit.

When it blows out

The Canal itself rarely blows out. If the tide is wrong, Scorton Creek is ten minutes east for the marsh game.

Bait

Mackerel and herring flushing through spring, sand eels and squid mid-season, adult menhaden pushing east tides in July.

Access (Public)

Free lots along the service road both banks. Fishing from a boat inside the land cut is prohibited, this is bank fishing only.

Wading (Bank only)

Never wade the Canal. Six knots of current will end your season and maybe you. Riprap casting only.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
