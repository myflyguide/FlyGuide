Camp Ellis & Saco Mouth

Saco ME · Maine · Striped Bass

Read the water

A mile of jetty pointing into the Saco River outflow. Bass hold the seam the whole length of it, and the walk out filters the crowd to the committed.

The cast

Work the river side of the jetty on the outgoing, casting up-current and swinging the seam. The far tip fishes best but the middle gets ignored and should not be.

Rig

9 or 10wt, 300 grain, herring flies in June, sand eels after, a stripping basket on the rocks.

Prime time

Outgoing tide at dawn or dusk, June through September.

When it blows out

East swell breaks over the jetty, stay off it. The inside river bends and Ferry Beach fish the same tide safely.

Bait

Alewives and herring out of the Saco in early summer, sand eels and silversides along the jetty, peanuts in fall.

Access (Public)

Camp Ellis pier lot at the end of Route 9. The long granite jetty and the river mouth are steps from the truck.

Wading (Bank and jetty)

Fish from the jetty rocks and the beach beside it. The Saco channel runs hard, keep your feet on granite or dry sand.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
