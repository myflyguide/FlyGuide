Wasque Point

Chappaquiddick, Marthas Vineyard MA · Cape & Islands · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The southeast corner of the Vineyard where the whole Muskeget Channel drags past a sand point. The classic island rip, bass in the sweep, albies and bonito in the fall.

The cast

Fish it like a river: cast up-current, one mend, swing the seam, step down. The take comes on the hang more often than not.

Rig

10wt earns its keep in the sweep, 300 grain, squid flies and sand eels, albie box from Labor Day.

Prime time

Moving tide at dawn, June, then September and October for the mixed bag.

When it blows out

South swell closes the point. East Beach up the outside or Cape Poge inside fish the same trip.

Bait

Sand eels and squid in the rip, bay anchovies in fall, bonito bait balls offshore in September.

Access (Ferry plus fee)

Chappy ferry from Edgartown, then Trustees lot at Wasque or OSV with permit. Tide times here are estimates off Nantucket, the rip has its own opinions.

Wading (Easy sand, brutal sweep)

Sand underfoot, but the Muskeget Channel sweep along the point is the strongest on the island. Knee deep maximum, and watch your line downstream.

Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
