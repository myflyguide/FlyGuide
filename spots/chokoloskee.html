Chokoloskee & Ten Thousand Islands

Everglades City FL · Everglades · Snook, Redfish, Tarpon

Read the water

Oyster bars, outside beaches, and pass edges that reload with every tide. Snook on the points, reds on the bars, tarpon in the passes May and June.

The cast

Fish the down-tide point of every island: cast past the point, strip through the seam. On the outside beaches, walk the bow and sight-cast cruisers in the first trough.

Rig

8wt floating, white baitfish flies and a Kwan for the reds, 30 to 40lb shock all day.

Prime time

Fall mullet run and the winter dry season lows. Summer mornings for the tarpon passes.

When it blows out

West wind stacks the outside. Slide one row of islands inside and the water goes calm and clean.

Bait

Pilchards and thread herring on the outside islands, mullet everywhere, shrimp on winter lows.

Access (Public ramps)

Chokoloskee island ramps and Everglades City. A thousand islands look identical, run a track out or hire a guide the first day, seriously.

Wading (No)

Oyster bars will shred boots and mud will keep them. Skiff fishery.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
