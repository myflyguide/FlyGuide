Key West Flats & Harbor

Key West FL · Lower Keys · Tarpon, Bonefish

Read the water

Year-round tarpon town. Winter warm spells wake the harbor fish, spring brings the migration through the basins, and the worm moons turn it electric.

The cast

Laid-up fish want the fly landed soft at eleven o'clock, three feet ahead, then barely moved. Swimming strings get the standard lead and slow strip. Do not hit them on the head.

Rig

12wt floating, tan Toads for clear sun, black/purple for grey light. 10wt does the harbor juveniles.

Prime time

March through June prime, but any winter week over 75 degrees can light the harbor up.

When it blows out

Twenty knots somewhere is a lee somewhere else, that is the gift of Key West. The guides just call it a running day.

Bait

Pilchards everywhere, crabs on the spring flood, shrimp bars at night, worms in season.

Access (Boat smart, some wade)

Harbor edges and Fort Zachary shoreline fish on foot. The basins, Boca Grande Key line, and the Marquesas are skiff water and worth every gallon.

Wading (Limited)

Smathers flat edges and the state park side wade at lower stages. Most of this fishery floats.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
