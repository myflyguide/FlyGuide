Sebastian Inlet

Sebastian FL · East Central FL · Snook, Tarpon, Redfish

Read the water

The east coast's fish funnel. Everything migrating trades through this cut, and the fall mullet run stacks snook and tarpon in the first trough like a parade.

The cast

Mullet run: dawn on the beach south of the jetty, cast parallel, big white fly swum with panic. Rest of year: night outgoing on the inside flats, shrimp fly on the shadow lines.

Rig

9wt intermediate, 40lb shock for snook, and a 11wt rigged when the tarpon crash the mullet pods.

Prime time

September and October beach mornings, otherwise outgoing tides at night.

When it blows out

Big swell owns the beach. The inside flats and Indian River spoil islands fish in anything.

Bait

The mullet run in September and October is biblical here, glass minnows summer, shrimp on winter night tides.

Access (Public, park fee)

State park both sides, famous north jetty, 24 hour fishing. The fly water is the beach corners and the flats inside, not elbow-to-elbow on the jetty.

Wading (Easy on the beach)

Beach and inside flats wade easy. The inlet current itself is deadly serious, stay off the rocks when swell runs.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
