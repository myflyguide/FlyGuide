Sandy Hook

Highlands NJ · New Jersey · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The hook tip rips, ocean bars, and bay flats in one walk, guarding New York Harbor. Spring bunker run puts the biggest bass of the year in casting range.

The cast

Spring: big flies to nervous bunker pods at dawn, let it fall through the school. Fall: run and gun the beach as bait pushes south, albies mixed in on the clean days.

Rig

10wt for spring bunker work with 400 grain and 8 inch Fleyes, 9wt intermediate for the fall sand eel game.

Prime time

May for giants, late September through November for numbers plus albies.

When it blows out

Ocean side blown means the bay side flats and Raritan rivers are in the lee, fish the flood there.

Bait

Adult bunker in spring with 40 pound bass under them, sand eels and rain bait in fall, mullet dropping out in September.

Access (Public, fee in season)

Gateway NRA lots, beach fee in summer, night fishing permit for after hours. The tip is a walk from Lot C.

Wading (Easy)

Easy sand ocean side. Bay side flats are soft in spots, test before you commit.

Live score, tides & 30-day board for Sandy Hook
Regulations change: NH Fish & Game saltwater · MA DMF recreational regs · RI DEM marine fisheries · NY DEC saltwater · NJ saltwater registry & regs · NC DMF regs (albies).
Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
