FLYGUIDE

Odiorne Point

Rye NH · NH Seacoast · Striped Bass

Read the water

A boulder flat where Berry Brook drains warm marsh water across cool coast. Bass slide on with the flood to root the rockweed, then stage on the outer boulders as it drops.

The cast

Work the brook outlet seam first, then fan the flat. Fish move in singles and pairs, so pick a lane and let them come. Sixty feet covers everything.

Rig

9wt, intermediate, olive/white Clouser 1/0. Gurgler over the flat at first light before the sun gets on it.

Prime time

Flood tide that peaks within two hours of dawn or dusk, light southwest wind.

When it blows out

Hard east wind dirties it fast. Bail to the Squamscott outgoing or the Piscataqua rips.

Bait

May juvenile herring, June to August sand eels and silversides, September peanut bunker along the rocks.

Access (Public, fee lot)

Odiorne Point State Park, day fee, gates dawn to dusk. Below mean high water is public trust in NH.

Wading (Easy)

The flat wades easy and fish push on with the flood. Top half of the tide is the game.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
