Homosassa

Homosassa FL · Nature Coast · Tarpon, Redfish

Read the water

Where the world records swam. May and June, strings of 150 pound fish track the outside grass flats in clear Gulf water, and everything about it is earned.

The cast

One shot fishing. See the string at 300 feet, set up the intercept, and make the one cast count: lead, sink, and a slow draw as the lead fish closes. Then hold on for the sleigh ride.

Rig

12wt, floating, 16 to 20lb class with 60lb shock if you care about records, black/purple early, tan when the sun is up.

Prime time

May through mid-June, first light through mid-morning, calm and clear or stay home.

When it blows out

Any real wind ends it, the fish are still there but you cannot see them. The backcountry reds around the river mouths save the day.

Bait

The giants here do not chase bait much, they are travelers. Crabs on the surface during big tides, needlefish and mullet for the reds inside.

Access (Boat)

MacRae's and the public ramp put you minutes from the flats. This is the giant tarpon grounds of legend, treat it like sacred water.

Wading (No)

Soft grass flats and long runs, a poling skiff fishery through and through.

Regulations change: FWC saltwater regs (snook stamp, tarpon C&R, slot limits).
Florida beaches are public below the mean high water line (the wet sand). Tarpon are catch and release only, fish over 40 inches stay in the water, that is the law. Snook takes a permit stamp and has seasons and slots that change by coast, check FWC before you keep anything.
