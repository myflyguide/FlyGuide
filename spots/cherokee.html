Cherokee Sound

Abaco, Bahamas · Abaco · Bonefish

Read the water

Classic DIY water: hard sand flats where singles and pairs slide on with the flood. Bigger and smarter than Marls fish, a proper test.

The cast

Walk slow, scan the edges of the light, and take the shot early. These fish come from deep water hungry on the young flood and leave when it drops.

Rig

8wt, 12ft leader to 12lb, tan Gotchas and Spawning Shrimp #4, small crab for the big singles.

Prime time

First half of the incoming, mid-morning sun, April through June and again October November.

When it blows out

East wind over 18 puts chop on it. The Casuarina side and the creek behind fish the lee.

Bait

Shrimp and small crabs on the sand, worms in the marl patches.

Access (DIY friendly)

Walk-in flats off the settlement and Casuarina Point, rental car water. Flats permit required, respect the guides working clients, give them the flat.

Wading (Easy)

Hard white sand wades like a sidewalk on the lower half of the tide. Watch your line of retreat as it floods.

Regulations change: Bahamas flats fishing regulations (license required, bones are C&R).
Since 2017 the Bahamas requires a flats fishing permit for everyone, and bonefish are catch and release. DIY wading is legal on most flats but some are guide-only by local agreement. Ask at the lodge or settlement before you walk somebody's home water, it buys you more than goodwill.
