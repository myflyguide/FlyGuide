FLYGUIDE

Lobsterville Beach

Aquinnah, Marthas Vineyard MA · Cape & Islands · Striped Bass, False Albacore

Read the water

The most fly-friendly beach in New England. Calm sound-side water, bait pinned against the shore, and the Menemsha jetty current next door.

The cast

Night game: unweighted squid fly on a slow swing, listen for pops. Day game in fall: albies run the beach at casting range, lead the melee and strip like you mean it.

Rig

9wt covers both. Floating for night squid, intermediate and small anchovy flies when the albies show.

Prime time

June nights around the squid, then September when albies and bass double up.

When it blows out

A north blow puts it in your face. Flip to the south shore ponds through the cuts.

Bait

Squid runs at night in early summer, sand eels all season, bay anchovies balled against the beach in September.

Access (Public, tight parking)

Limited roadside on Lobsterville Rd, or bike from the Menemsha bike ferry. Tide here runs hours off Boston, treat times as estimates.

Wading (Easy)

Gentle sand and gravel the whole stretch, the friendliest wade in the Northeast.

Tideline law varies hard up here. In MA and ME the intertidal zone is private to the low water mark, but fishing and fowling access is protected under the colonial ordinance. NH, RI, CT, NY, NJ: public trust below mean high water. When in doubt, stay wet.
